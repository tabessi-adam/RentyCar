\chapter*{Conclusion and Perspectives}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion and Perspectives}
\markboth{Conclusion and Perspectives}{}

The objective of our project was to provide a comprehensive solution for car rental management through the "RentyCar" system. This system is designed to streamline the car rental process, enhance user experience, and provide efficient management tools for rental businesses. By implementing modern web technologies and following best practices, we have created a robust platform that addresses the challenges of traditional car rental operations.

Throughout our work, we adopted a structured development methodology that allowed us to follow the complete evolution of the project through all stages. By analyzing existing car rental systems, we identified key areas for improvement such as reservation management, vehicle tracking, and user experience. Our solution overcomes these challenges by implementing features like real-time availability checking, automated reservation processing, and comprehensive vehicle management.

The success of the RentyCar system opens the door to various future perspectives for its improvement and expansion. These interesting avenues to explore include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Development of a mobile application for on-the-go reservations and management
    \item Integration of payment gateways for seamless transactions
    \item Implementation of AI-powered features for dynamic pricing and vehicle recommendations
    \item Expansion of the system to support multiple languages and currencies
    \item Integration with third-party services for additional features
\end{itemize}

In summary, our final year project represents a significant advancement in car rental management systems. By leveraging modern web technologies and following best practices in software development, we have created a scalable and maintainable solution that can be enriched through future improvements and perspectives in this evolving field.
